---
baslik: After the Rain Stops
---
One morning you wake up and someone is beside you. Their hair spread across the pillow, mouth slightly open, perhaps snoring a little. Not like in the films. No light filters through the curtain onto their face, no music plays. Just a person, a real person, sleeping. And in that moment, with no one watching, without saying a word, you choose them once more.

That is what choosing is. It is not standing before a door one day and saying "all right, this one." It is opening that door again every morning. It does not stay open today just because you opened it yesterday. Every day you have to put your hand on the handle and turn it. Some days it turns easily, some days it sticks as if rusted. But you turn it anyway.

Falling in love is not like that. Falling in love happens to you. One day in a café, in a message, in a glance, it falls into you when you are not ready at all. You do nothing; it does you. Your heart races, sleep leaves you, your face flushes when you hear their name. It is beautiful, but it is not yours. It is like rain; it comes and it goes.

Choosing is what remains after the rain stops. It is going on walking down the wet street.

The person you choose is not the flawless one you built in your head. They never will be. Grumpy in the morning, tired in the evening, sometimes hurt by meaningless things, sometimes silent right when you need them. They drink their coffee the way you don't like. They leave the dishes on the edge of the sink. They sing a song with the wrong words and get cross when you correct them. That is who you choose. Not the one in your dreams, but the one in front of you. With their broken places, their odd habits, their bad days.

And you let something go. No one says this, but choosing is also giving up. You turn your back on the faces passing you in the street, on the possibilities in your phone, on that voice whispering "maybe there's someone better somewhere." Perhaps that voice never falls completely silent. But you don't listen to it. Because you know that someone who keeps searching for better can never fully love anything. It always stays half.

Love as a feeling comes and goes. Some evenings you look at them and nothing stirs inside you. Only tiredness, only habit. Those are the evenings when the real choice is made. Pouring their tea even when the feeling isn't there. Asking about their day even when you don't feel like it. Listening once more even when your patience is gone. While the heart is silent, the hands keep talking. And after a while, without your noticing, the heart begins to talk again too.

And there is fear. When you choose someone you give them a key. They can open your door, come inside, see you at your most defenceless. And whenever they want, they can break you with that key. You know this. Perhaps you have been broken before. Your wounds still ache when the weather changes. But you hold out the key anyway. Because a house whose door is always locked, however safe it is, in the end becomes a tomb.

And the hardest part is not holding on to them. Choosing is not possessing. Holding their hand but not gripping their wrist. Standing beside them but not in front of them. Letting them, too, choose you anew every morning. Because someone held by force is not with you, only next to you. But someone who stays because they chose you is someone who stays inside even though they find the door open every day. There is no greater declaration of love than that.

Perhaps this is love at its quietest, least poetic. No one writes songs about it. No one makes a film called "today I chose them again." But most of life is exactly this: an ordinary Tuesday evening, two people in a kitchen, one washing the dishes, the other drying. They aren't even talking. But they are both there. Both of them, with no one forcing them, have chosen to be there.

One day, looking back, perhaps you won't remember the big moments. The first date, the first kiss, those exciting nights. What you will remember is thousands of ordinary mornings. And in each of them, with no one watching, that small choice you made again.

Falling in love was something that happened to you. Loving them is something you do, every morning when you open your eyes.
